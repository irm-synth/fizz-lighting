fizzctl
-------
https://github.com/ayoubdya/fizzctl   (PyPI: fizzctl; package author listed as Ayoub Dya)

fizz-gui.ps1 contains a PowerShell port of knowledge from fizzctl: the HID report IDs and
sequences for the Redragon K617 Fizz, the captured firmware frames (embedded as base64), the
effect table, the per-key and canvas layouts, the Cfg.ini keymap block encoder and the
virtual-key to HID table. fizzctl is distributed under the MIT License, reproduced here
verbatim from the upstream repository:
